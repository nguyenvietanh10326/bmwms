UC22: Confirm Warehouse Receipt Goal: Allows Warehouse Staff to confirm actual received quantity, lot, expiry information, and item condition. Field Value Type UI Primary Actor Warehouse Staff Secondary Actor(s) Purchasing Staff, Warehouse Manager Module M04 - Inbound Management Business Group Receiving Execution FT-ID Ref FT-22 Depends On UC20 Preconditions An active user is signed in and holds one of the allowed fixed roles. The selected Inbound Order is READY or RECEIVING and is assigned or available for warehouse execution. Postconditions Actual receipt details are confirmed; the Inbound Order is RECEIVED when every planned line has a receipt decision. Main Flow [U] Warehouse Staff opens an eligible Inbound Order and starts receiving. [S] The system locks the current execution version, changes READY to RECEIVING, and displays the planned lines. [U] Warehouse Staff enters actual delivered quantity, accepted quantity, damaged quantity, lot number, manufacture/expiry information when required, and condition notes for each line. [S] The system validates quantities, lot and expiry rules, duplicate lots, and the relationship to the planned lines. [U] Warehouse Staff confirms the receipt after reviewing the calculated missing and damaged quantities. [S] The system saves immutable receipt execution details, records the actor/time, changes the order to RECEIVED when complete, and notifies the relevant roles. Alternative Flows AF-01 - Missing quantity: Actual delivered quantity is below expected quantity. The system records the actual receipt and missing quantity, completes the receipt decision, and makes the shortage available for a supplemental Inbound Order; it does not reopen the receipt. AF-02 - Damaged goods: Some delivered quantity is damaged. Warehouse Staff records the damaged quantity and condition. The system retains the receipt evidence and makes the replacement quantity available for a supplemental Inbound Order. AF-03 - Required lot or expiry missing: The product group requires lot or expiry control but a value is absent or invalid. The system blocks confirmation for that line and identifies the missing data. AF-04 - Partial working save: Warehouse Staff saves progress without final confirmation. The order remains RECEIVING and no final inventory transaction is posted. AF-05 - Concurrent execution: Another staff member is already confirming the same version. The system blocks the second confirmation and reloads the latest execution state. Business Rules BR-01: Actual delivered, accepted, and damaged quantities must be zero or greater, and accepted + damaged may not exceed delivered quantity. BR-02: FEFO-controlled products require a lot number and a valid future expiry date; FIFO products require a received date or equivalent sequence (see PRD DR-05 and GB-05). BR-03: Missing or damaged quantities are handled through a supplemental Inbound Order referencing the original Purchase Order and Inbound Order (see PRD DR-08). BR-04: Receipt confirmation is atomic and writes immutable execution and audit data (see PRD GB-11 and GB-20). BR-05: No Invoice or accounting Goods Receipt is created by this use case (see PRD DR-19). Request Fields - WarehouseReceiptConfirmationRequest Field Type Rule Inbound Order Text Required. Expiry date Date Required for expiry-controlled products; must be after receipt date. Condition Choice Required. ACCEPTED, DAMAGED, or MIXED. Condition notes Text Required when damage or shortage exists; maximum 1,000 characters. On success: Inbound Order Details shows actual quantities, lots, conditions, receipt actor/time, and RECEIVED status when complete. Allowed Roles System Administrator, Warehouse Manager, Warehouse Staff Verification Criteria Given When Then a READY Inbound Order exists with valid planned lines Warehouse Staff submits valid actual receipt information for every line the actual receipt is stored once, shortage and damage are recorded, the order becomes RECEIVED, and no accounting Goods Receipt is created Additional scenarios to cover in tests: Confirm a complete undamaged receipt.
